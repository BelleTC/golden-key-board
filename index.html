How to read this board

Sources

  • Weekly Online Reputation Standing reports, one per property, from the Review Reports library on SharePoint.
  • Review Rally and Golden Key figures are pulled directly from each week's rating and review-count tables.
  • Individual Recognition mentions are text-mined from the guest reviews quoted in the same reports.

What's estimated, not final

  • Named-mention counts are a first pass, not a certified tally — spelling variants (Anika / Annika, Marco / Marko, Ariane / Arianne) were merged by best judgment. GM sign-off still governs actual payout, as the program specifies.
  • Golden Key points use the pre-launch week (Aug 3–9) as the quarter's starting line, since full scoring went live Aug 10.
  • Cycle 1 (Aug 10–Sep 6) is closed and archived under "Past Cycles" above; Cycle 2 (Sep 7–Oct 4) is live, currently at week 3 of 4 for all three properties. The Vivere Golden Key is not tied to that cycle boundary — it kept accumulating straight through the Cycle 1 → Cycle 2 rollover and will keep doing so through Cycle 3, closing only at the 12‑report quarter mark.

Refresh

  • This board refreshes automatically each week as new reports are filed, pulling straight from SharePoint.
  • Rotation ahead for Platform of the Quarter: Booking.com (Q4 2026) → Agoda (Q1 2027) → TripAdvisor (Q2 2027) → Expedia (Q3 2027), then repeats.
  • The Vivere Review Rally and Individual Recognition run on the shorter clock: each cycle covers 4 weekly reports, and once every property has filed its week‑4 report, the cycle closes — its final standing is archived under "Past Cycles" above and both boards reset to zero for the new cycle.
  • The Vivere Golden Key runs on a longer, quarterly clock: it takes 12 weekly reports (3 full 4‑report cycles) before a winner is declared and points reset. It is unaffected by the Rally / Individual Recognition reset at each cycle boundary — its points, per-platform ledger, and rating history all carry forward from Cycle 1 through Cycle 3 of the quarter.
